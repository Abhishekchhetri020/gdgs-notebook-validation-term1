The one thing to take from this audit: a tick tells you a notebook was produced. It does not tell you the subject teacher had checked it, whether the work was up to date, or when a teacher last saw it. That gap is why Director Ma'am wrote on 29 July, “the validation is not solving our purpose”, and why Office Order GDGPSD/2026-27/OFFICE/042 was issued on 30 July adding Last checked on (date) / unchecked and Complete / Incomplete columns. Reports written before that date cannot answer those questions retrospectively. Under her instruction of 30 July, “Incomplete and not aligned with the requirements to be considered as NOT SUBMITTED.”

What the marks on the sheets mean

Reading a sheet correctly depends on this vocabulary — and on treating an empty box as a real signal rather than a pass.

✓ / CompleteNotebook produced and accepted by the validator. Does NOT certify that the subject teacher had checked it.
NS / Not Submitted / Not BroughtStudent produced no notebook. Written NS (AB) when the cause was absence, NS (Not Brought) when the child was present.
AB / AbsentStudent absent that day. Legitimate — but the notebook is still unchecked and must be re-run.
NC / T.N.C. / Not CheckedTEACHER LAPSE. The work was done; the subject teacher never checked or signed it.
INC / Incomplete / W.I.C.Student lapse — work not up to date, pages or exercises missing.
N.M. — Index not madeIndex page never started. N.C. on Mr. Abhaydeep's sheets means 'index not completed'.
Index / Date / P.G. missingMaintenance defects recorded in the Remarks column.
Left / New / TransferredStudent has left, joined recently, or moved section. Roster correction, not a defect.
(blank)NO VERDICT RECORDED. Treat as NOT VALIDATED — a blank block of rows means those notebooks never reached the validator.
